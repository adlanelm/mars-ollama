All benchmark claims for both are vendor-reported. Method: `model-ab.sh` with `ab-prompts.json`, as in [turbo-2026-10-21.md](turbo-2026-10-21.md). Each prompt runs once
greedy for 400 tok, then once sampled for up to 16k tok. There is one sample per prompt, so thinking lengths are noisy.

- **MoE pair:** qwen3.6 production flags (`-c 131072 -t 8 -tb 8 -ub 2048 -b 2048`, `--fit` expert split). qwen3.6 samples at temp 1 / top_p 0.95 / top_k 20 /
  presence 1.5. Ornith uses its card's general-task values, temp 0.6 / top_p 0.95.
- **Dense pair:** qwen3.8-27b production flags (`-ub 256`) at `-c 49152`, plus Glimmer at `-c 131072`. Glimmer samples at temp 1 / top_p 0.95 /
  top_k 64 with the system prompt `Reasoning strength: high` (the card recommends high for coding and agentic work, and Meta's table uses it).

Vision and tool calling were not tested.

Quality: every run got every checkable answer right: 403, the pet puzzle, the two_sum fix (run against 4 asserts), and the Fibonacci doctests pass.
Every Unity answer contains a MonoBehaviour. These prompts can't tell the models apart on quality.

- **Ornith vs qwen3.6:** about 6% faster per token. It thinks about 1/3 as much: qwen3.6 thought 1.2k–2.5k tokens even on the arithmetic and Fibonacci prompts, against
  Ornith's 170–630. That gets the answer about 2.1x sooner. The answers are longer (more formatting).
- **Glimmer vs qwen3.8-27b:** same speed (+4%), and **128k context fully on the GPU with 2 GiB to spare**, where qwen3.8 has 48k with 1.1 GiB.
  It thinks more on short prompts (330–990 vs 70–450 tok) and less on the Unity prompt. In total it is about 20% slower to the answer on this set.
- qwen3.8-27b at 48k ran at 32.4 tok/s today, against 22 tok/s on 2026-10-21. The slowdown in turbo-2026-10-21.md was transient.

## Verdict

Both are worth a real-use trial, as extra llama-swap entries next to the current models:
- **Ornith:** a candidate to replace qwen3.6 (same size, faster to the answer).
- **Glimmer:** a candidate to replace qwen3.8-27b where long context matters (128k instead of 48k at the same speed). Its mmproj (Q8_0, 2.05 GB) would
  run on the CPU like qwen3.8's.

Check tool calling (`smoke.sh`), vision, and pi/opencode use before switching defaults.
