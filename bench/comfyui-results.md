# ComfyUI on ROCm results (Phase 6)

Measured 2026-10-11 on the RX 7800 XT (gfx1101, 16 GB), 30 GiB RAM, via `bin/gpu-mode comfy` (LLMs unloaded, aux reranker about 370 MiB resident). Timings come from `bin/comfy-bench` (HTTP API, 1024x1024, seeds change per run, run 1 = warm-up, later runs timed). Workflows: [../comfyui/workflows/](../comfyui/workflows/), generated by `make_workflows.py` from the official `workflow_templates` nodes and defaults (the Krea graph omits the optional LoRA and prompt-expansion branch). Prompt used (synthetic): "low-poly rusty maintenance robot, isometric view, 1999 video game render".

## Issues and findings

- **`PYTORCH_HIP_ALLOC_CONF=expandable_segments:True` breaks generation here.** About 12 GB of VRAM is held outside torch's accounting ("1.44 GiB allocated by PyTorch") and VAEDecode OOMs allocating 4.5 GiB at 1024^2. `/free` does not release it. Reproduced with and without `--disable-dynamic-vram`; removing the variable fixes it. Not set in the image. (The brief's env list included it.)
- **Krea 2 Turbo does not reach 30 s** (about 40 s). Cause is the 12.5 GB fp8 model: RDNA3 has no fp8 matmul, so weights are upcast, and about 4 s/it. Options not tried: bf16/other quant, fewer steps, resolution 896.
- **Host RAM and disk contention matter.** Z-Image first measured 99-210 s while another host job (a 186 GB model download) saturated the disk and RAM was tight (ComfyUI RSS reached 19 GB with default caching and pinned memory). With `--disable-pinned-memory --cache-ram 6` and a quiet disk it dropped to 26-32 s. Keep other heavy I/O off while benchmarking.
- Model repos moved: `Comfy-Org/flux2-klein-4B` redirects to `Comfy-Org/vae-text-encorder-for-flux-klein-4b`, which still holds the same files (diffusion model 7,751,105,712 bytes). `models.txt` points at the new name, so the `black-forest-labs/FLUX.2-klein-4B` fallback was not needed. `Comfy-Org/Krea2` also redirects (to `Comfy-Org/Krea-2`) and works through the redirect.
- First `comfy-pull` run: a stalled `hf download` was restarted once; downloads resume. A rerun skips everything (idempotent). Total about 75 GB in `~/llm/comfyui/models`.
- PSX style LoRA (Civitai): skipped by choice (2026-10-11). The PS1 look comes from the Unity PSX layer; a LoRA would only restyle concept art. Optional later: own style LoRA.
- Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 GGUF and its text encoder are downloaded but not speed-tested (ComfyUI-GGUF loads in the log).
- `gpu-mode llm` freed VRAM without `--stop`; `--stop` also works and was used at the end.

## Krea 2 steps/resolution sweep (2026-10-11)

Krea 2 Turbo fp8 (`krea2_turbo.json`: euler, simple, cfg 1, same model files), 12 synthetic game-asset prompts (`comfyui/sweeps/krea_prompts.txt`) x 2 seeds (1234, 5678) x 4 variants = 96 images, plus one discarded warm-up (54 s). Run with `bin/comfy-sweep run`, grouped by variant, prompt-major (the second seed of a prompt reuses the cached text conditioning; same order for every variant). GPU time about 49 min. Nothing else heavy was running (colibri unloaded by `gpu-mode comfy`).

| Variant | Settings | s/image mean (min-max) | New prompt | Same prompt, next seed | Peak VRAM (card) | Peak host RAM used | Peak container RSS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| s8-1024 (baseline) | 8 steps, 1024x1024 | **39.1** (37.2-41.4) | 40.1 | 38.2 | 15.9 GiB | 17.5 GiB | 16.4 GiB* |
| s6-1024 | 6 steps, 1024x1024 | **29.1** (28.0-30.4) | 29.9 | 28.4 | 15.8 GiB | 18.5 GiB | 16.4 GiB* |
| s4-1024 | 4 steps, 1024x1024 | **20.0** (19.1-21.1) | 20.7 | 19.3 | 15.8 GiB | 18.6 GiB | 9.9 GiB |
| s8-896 | 8 steps, 896x896 | **29.9** (28.6-30.9) | 30.7 | 29.1 | 15.6 GiB | 18.1 GiB | 9.9 GiB |

Wall clock per image (submit to saved file). VRAM is whole-card `mem_info_vram_used` (includes desktop and the aux reranker). Host RAM is MemTotal - MemAvailable for the whole machine. Container RSS from `docker stats` (1 s samples); *the 16.4 GiB in the first two variants is the model-load peak carried over from the start of the run, later variants show the steady state of about 10 GiB.

Memory: the step count does not change VRAM or RAM (same weights, same activations per step); time scales almost linearly at about 4.8 s per step plus about 1 s fixed. 896x896 lowers activation memory and time per step (about 3.7 s vs 4.8 s at 1024), so 8 steps at 896 costs about the same as 6 steps at 1024. All variants keep the card full because ComfyUI keeps the 12.5 GB model resident.

Finding: every image (also at 896) logs a HIP OOM at VAEDecode (`trying to allocate 3.4 GiB`, about 2.1 GiB free) and ComfyUI recovers (its VAE decode retries tiled on OOM); every image still succeeded. Avoiding the failed first attempt (e.g. a tiled VAE decode node) might save a little time per image (not tried).

**Contact sheet:** `xdg-open ~/llm/comfyui/output/krea-sweep/index.html` (or `file:///home/adlan/llm/comfyui/output/krea-sweep/index.html`). Blind mode is on by default (columns shuffled per row with a fixed seed, labelled A-D, "Reveal" shows the variants); a PS1 preview toggle shows 256 px / RGB555 Bayer dither / 64-colour versions (`comfyui/sweeps/ps1_preview.py`, run with `uvx --with pillow`); picks are kept in localStorage and "Export picks" downloads a JSON with the letter-to-variant mapping. Rebuild with `bin/comfy-sweep sheet`. Raw numbers: `results.json` in the same directory.

**Quality verdict (user, blind, 2026-10-11):** one pick per row, 24 rows (`picks.json` next to the sheet).

| Variant | Picks | s/image |
|---|---|---|
| s8-1024 | 8 | 39 |
| s6-1024 | 7 | 29 |
| s8-896 | 6 | 30 |
| s4-1024 | 3 | 20 |

8 / 7 / 6 is within chance for 24 picks (random picking gives about 6 each); only 4 steps fell behind, weakly. By category: characters and props leaned 8 steps, textures 896² (3 of 4), the UI icon 6 steps (2 of 2).

**Decision:** default `krea2_turbo.json` to **6 steps at 1024²**. That's no visible loss in the blind test, it meets the 30 s target, and 1024² is kept for textures and turnarounds. Use 8 steps for final hero renders. 8 steps at 896² costs the same time as 6 at 1024² and gains nothing.

## Tiled VAE decode (2026-10-11)

Question: does `VAEDecodeTiled` remove the OOM-then-retry at the end of every Krea 2 Turbo image, and is it faster? Setup: `krea2_turbo.json` (6 steps, 1024², euler/simple, cfg 1). 6 synthetic prompts (char-android, creature-flesh, prop-terminal, tex-floorplate, env-corridor, ui-icon from `krea_prompts.txt`), seed 1234. One discarded warm-up per variant. Run with `bin/comfy-sweep run --name krea-tiled --variant plain:steps=6,tile=0 | tiled-512:steps=6,tile=512,overlap=64 | tiled-768:steps=6,tile=768,overlap=64`. GPU time was about 13 min. Docling and granite-vision were idle during the runs; an earlier attempt was stopped for a PDF import and its numbers were discarded.

| Variant | s/image mean (min-max) | OOM lines in log (7 images incl. warm-up) | Peak VRAM (card) | Pixel diff vs plain, mean / max (0-255) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| plain `VAEDecode` | **30.1** (28.7-31.9) | 7 (every image) | 15.8 GiB | - |
| tiled 512 / overlap 64 | 31.2 (29.4-32.5) | **0** | 15.8 GiB | 0.13 / 46 (99th percentile 1) |
| tiled 768 / overlap 64 | 30.5 (28.1-32.3) | 7 (every image) | 15.8 GiB | 0.11 / 35 (99th percentile 1) |

- Tile 512 removes the OOM. Tile 768 still tries to allocate too much and falls back exactly like plain.
- The output is the same in practice. ComfyUI's fallback after the OOM is itself a tiled decode, so the plain images were already tiled-decoded. The difference is mostly 0-1 levels. A diff image amplified 8x only shows a faint grid along the different overlap bands (around 450-520 px and 900 px). No seams are visible in the tiled-512 images (checked tex-floorplate and env-corridor at x/y = 512).
- Speed: the failed first attempt costs almost nothing. Tiled 512 is about 1 s/image slower (the ranges overlap; n = 6). Peak VRAM is the same: the card stays full because the 12.5 GB model stays resident.

**Decision:** keep plain `VAEDecode` as the default. Tiled 512 is not faster, and the OOM line is only a harmless log warning. The generator has `KREA_VAE_TILE` (0 = plain, N = `VAEDecodeTiled` with tile size N, overlap 64). `comfy-sweep` variants accept `tile=T[,overlap=O]` (`tile=0` = plain). Switch to 512 if the OOM path ever starts failing, for example with less free VRAM or larger images. Outputs and `results.json` are in `~/llm/comfyui/output/krea-tiled/`.
