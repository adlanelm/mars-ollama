# Docling: plain docling-serve vs docling-proxy, picture-description models (2026-10-16)

docling-serve **1.37.0** (`docling-serve-rocm:main` rebuilt 2026-10-16, commit 8e0cdd1; jobkit 3.8.2, docling 2.138.0), llama-swap v264-vulkan-b11580.
Inputs are synthetic only: `bench/make-synthetic-pdf.py` (seed 42), 10 pages (tables on even pages, vector bar charts on pages 3/6/9) and 200 pages.
Requests use Open WebUI's form fields (sync `/v1/convert/file`, `image_export_mode=placeholder`, `\f` page breaks, md+json).
No chat model loaded and ComfyUI stopped during the runs. Desktop baseline is 0.73 GiB VRAM.

## Verdict

- **Keep docling-proxy.** A plain long-lived docling-serve fails the decision rule for two reasons:
  - **VRAM:** docling-serve keeps about 2.2 GiB idle after its first conversion. With that held, qwen3.8-27b no longer fits on the GPU: **15.0 tok/s instead of 32.4** (same prompt, measured back to back).
  - **RAM:** every 200-page PDF raises the resting RAM, and it never comes back down: 1.95 → 2.9 → 3.07 → 3.34 GiB after three of them. Small PDFs level off at about 1.95 GiB.
- The proxy keeps VRAM at the baseline after every job, and its RAM goes back to about 0.5 GiB once the warm child drains. The cost is about 8 s of child start-up per file: 10.4 s vs 2.0 s for 10 pages, and 66 s vs 36 s for 200 pages, which the proxy splits into 4 chunks.
- **Picture description: Granite Vision 4.1 4B via llama-swap** is now the server default (`DOCLING_SERVE_DEFAULT_PICTURE_DESCRIPTION_PRESET`).
  - Granite Vision 3.3 only says "a bar chart with values". 4.1 reads labels and most values, and it is faster.
  - Running 3.3 locally in docling (transformers) gives the same weak text and leaves about 7 GiB of VRAM held by docling.
