# Qwen3.8-27B GSQ-RCO IQ3_S vs stock UD-Q3_K_XL, 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-24

Candidate: `ISTA-DASLab/Qwen3.8-27B-GSQ-RCO-GGUF` IQ3_S (11.77 GB, per-tensor mixed precision). Baseline: `qwen3.8-27b`,
`unsloth` UD-Q3_K_XL (13.15 GB). llama-swap v265-vulkan-b11804, production flags (`-ub 256 --no-mmproj-offload`, q8_0 KV),
stock's `mmproj-F16` for both. Result: **both kept; pi uses GSQ** (`qwen3.8-27b-gsq`, 64k).

## Quality

Perplexity on a corpus from this repo: stock 4.149, IQ3_S 4.193 (+1.1%), IQ3_XXS 4.311 (+3.9%); MTP builds equal their
plain builds. KL divergence not measured (BF16 is 54 GB).

`bench/suite.py` (30 tasks x 2 samples, 16k max tokens), `reasoning_effort` per run:

| | low | medium | xhigh (template default) |
|---|---|---|---|
| stock | 97% | 100% | 88% |
| GSQ IQ3_S | 98% | 93% | 87% |

- Differences between the builds are within noise at 2 samples. Only code separates runs.
- xhigh loses all its points to 16k truncation (7/60 for both): of the answers that finished, stock xhigh scored 100% and
  GSQ xhigh 98%. Low writes about half the tokens of xhigh (1.3-1.6k vs 2.8k per task).
- Vision (OCR, shapes, table, spatial; 4 images x 2 samples, low): both 8/8.
