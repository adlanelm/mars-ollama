# Qwen3.8-27B TURBO (DavidAU) vs stock qwen3.8-27b, 2026-10-21

at revision `b9bce7e8`. This is a community merge, uncensored ("Heretic"), and its benchmark claims are self-reported. The card says it uses 1/2–1/10 of
stock Qwen3.8's thinking tokens. The repo has no Q3_K_XL, so the Q3 tier is IQ3_M:

Baseline: `unsloth/Qwen3.8-27B-GGUF` UD-Q3_K_XL (13.15 GB, 12.24 GiB), the current `qwen3.8-27b`. The files sit in
`~/llm/models/DavidAU/…`; they are not in `models.lock.yaml` and not in llama-swap.

Method: `turbo-ab.sh` (lsbench container, qwen3.8-27b production flags, `--fit`), `turbo-prompts.json`
(verify-mtp prompts plus a logic puzzle and a two_sum bug-find). Each prompt runs twice: greedy for 400 tok, then sampled for up to 16k tok.
Stock samples at temp 1 / top_p 0.95 / top_k 20. TURBO samples at the card's temp 0.7, rep pen 1, with the rest the same. MTP uses `--spec-type draft-mtp
--spec-draft-n-max 2`, with the heads embedded and no `-md`. Thinking tokens come from server `/tokenize` of `reasoning_content`. There is one sample per
prompt, so per-prompt thinking lengths are noisy: stock's Unity prompt took 9.2k and then 4.4k thinking tokens across the two runs.

## Fit on the GPU

| | 48k ctx (production) | 12k ctx |
|---|---|---|
| stock UD-Q3_K_XL | 66/66 layers, 13.7 GiB projected, tg ~22 tok/s | 66/66, tg 32.4 |
| TURBO IQ3_M | **58/65**, tg 13.2 | 65/65 (1.25 GiB left), tg 30.1 |
| TURBO MTP-IQ3_M | **50/66**, tg 10–12 | **56/66**, tg 14–17 |

TURBO only fits fully on the GPU at about 12k context. The MTP file doesn't fit at any useful context: it needs 14.8 GiB at 12k, and about 14.1 GiB is free.
MTP acceptance was good (75–95%, mean length about 2.6), but the CPU-resident layers cost more than the drafts save.

Almost all of the saving comes from the open-ended Unity prompt (stock 4.4k–9.2k thinking tok, TURBO 0.25k–1.7k). On the short
prompts TURBO thinks **more** than stock: arithmetic 252 vs 87, puzzle 300 vs 119, at 12k.

Quality: every run got every checkable answer right: 17×23+144/12 = 403, the pet puzzle, the two_sum fix (check before insert), and the
Fibonacci doctests pass. The Unity scripts are comparable (grid snap with `Mathf.Round` and iso↔world conversion).

## Verdict

TURBO gets the answer about 1.6x faster at the same context, mostly by cutting long thinking. But at the Q3 tier it costs 0.9 GiB
more than UD-Q3_K_XL, and that **drops the dense 27B from 48k to about 12k context** if it is to stay fully on the GPU. At 48k it spills and is
slower than stock per token. The MTP variant spills at every context on 16 GB. Keep stock `qwen3.8-27b` as the default. If
fast short-context chat is wanted, TURBO IQ3_M could be a separate 12k entry. IQ2_M (11.7 GB) would fit at 48k but is a lower quant tier.

Side finding: stock gets 32 tok/s at 12k vs about 22 tok/s at 48k, with all layers on the GPU in both. That is worth checking separately (the 48k config
leaves only about 1.1 GiB free).
